# Data dictionary

Probabilities and fidelities are dimensionless. Numerical arrays use double
precision; symbolic entries retain rational and algebraic expressions.
All files in `data/` are generated by `python reproduce.py data` from the
source parameters in `scripts/build_data.py`.

## Conditional-state arrays

`POLICY_p1_c0_5.npz`, `POLICY_p0_9_c0_5.npz`, and `POLICY_p1_c0_9.npz`
use the midpoint internal basis. The data density matrices are
`A=[[(1+pc)/2, p*sqrt(1-c*c)/2], [p*sqrt(1-c*c)/2, (1-pc)/2]]`
and B has the opposite off-diagonals. The helper is `H+=diag(1,0)` for U4
and `H-=diag(0,1)` for U3. The source Bloch length is p; purity is `(1+p*p)/2`.

- `U`: 5-by-5 four-mixing unitary; matrices act rightmost first.
- `source_states`: A, B, H+, shape (3,2,2).
- `occupations`: all 35 three-photon records, shape (35,5). The order comes
  from lexicographic nondecreasing lists of occupied output slots, converted
  to counts at ports 0-4; it is not lexicographic count order.
- `J`: 35 unnormalized 4-by-4 reference matrices in basis (00,01,10,11).
  Their sum is I4/4; each matrix traces over all unresolved internal labels.
- `p`, `n`: raw probabilities and maximum local-unitary Bell weights.
- `targets`: maximizing maximally entangled kets, shape (35,4).
- `recoveries`: W on the first reference, shape (35,2,2). For coefficient
  matrix Psi of the target ket, `W=sqrt(2)*Psi.conj().T`.
- `three_U`, `three_sources`, `three_raw_J`, `three_p`, `three_n`,
  `three_targets`, `three_recoveries`: corresponding U3/H- arrays.
- `three_ceiling_acceptance`: complete policy at U3's highest quality.
- `source_bloch_length`, `pure_component_overlap`, `metadata_json`: source
  scalars and optical, basis, probability, recovery and degeneracy conventions.

A degenerate maximum uses the first nonzero column of its real magic-basis
spectral projector; null records use Phi+ and identity recovery.

`TWO_HELPER_p1_c0_9.npz` contains the six-mode unitary, four independent source
density matrices A,B,H-,H-, all 126 four-photon occupations, raw J matrices,
and the selected record (1,1,0,0,1,1). This point has ideal fidelity one.

## Exact records and policies

`EXACT_RECORDS_SYMMETRIC_QUBIT.json` gives U4 conditional matrices and real
magic-basis eigenvalues in `tau=Tr(AB)=(1-p*p)/2+p*p*c*c` and
`q=Tr((A+B)H)=1+p*c*h_z`, for an axial helper with |h_z|<=1.
`RECORD_GROUPS_SYMMETRIC_QUBIT.json` collects the 14 equal-probability and
equal-spectrum classes of Appendix E. Class multiplicity counts raw records;
recoveries remain record dependent. `magic_eigenvalues` maps expressions to
eigenvalue multiplicity.

`FIXED_NETWORK_FRONTIERS.json` contains complete fixed-control frontier samples,
all policy breakpoints, attained endpoints, and fractional policies at selected
targets for U4/H+ and U3/H-. Acceptance arrays follow the occupation order above.
`target_F`, `P` and `N` are the target, accepted probability and Bell weight;
when P>0, the accepted fidelity is N/P. P=0 denotes an infeasible target.
The constraint concerns the average accepted ensemble, so a policy can include
individual records below its target fidelity.

## Physical comparison curves

`FIGURE4_PLOT_INPUTS.json` uses pure sources at c=0.9. Each curve contains
`[target_F, P_attempt]` pairs: complete bare, U3/H- and U4/H+ policies,
common-center Gaussian filters, and independent-center equal-width Gaussian
filters. The Gaussian curves run from the bare quality ceiling to F=1.
The common-center endpoint has zero transmission; the independent-center
endpoint is finite. `two_helper_point` and `rank_one_point` are feasible
unit-fidelity points and meet every lower target at the same probability.

The efficiencies are sA=sB=0.8, sH=0.7 per helper, path transmission 0.95,
detector efficiency 0.9, and power transmission 0.98 per padded layer.
Probability per source attempt includes these factors. Other probabilities
are per supplied trial, with ideal reference-rail Bell inputs.

## Generated results and figures

`results/numerical_results.json` contains representative fidelities, probabilities,
thresholds and physical comparisons. `results/figure3_boundary.csv` contains
`c,p_crit`; at c=0 and c=1 the plotted threshold uses the limiting value one.
Figures 1-4 are generated as PDF, SVG and PNG. Figure 1 is the optical layout;
Figure 2 is the pure-source comparison; Figure 3 is the mixed-source boundary
and fixed-instrument frontier; Figure 4 is the physical rate comparison.
